# Demo Institute of Technology (DEMO_ONLY) — Attendance, Grading & Promotion

## Attendance Rules
A student must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course to be eligible for
the end-semester examination (DEMO_ONLY value). Attendance between 65% and 75%
may be condoned only with an approved medical certificate.

## Grading Rules
The institute uses a 10-point grading scale (DEMO_ONLY):
- O (Outstanding) = 10 grade points
- A (Excellent) = 9 grade points
- B (Good) = 8 grade points
- C (Average) = 7 grade points
- P (Pass) = 5 grade points
- F (Fail) = 0 grade points

The SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points in a semester, and the
CGPA is the cumulative credit-weighted average across all completed semesters.

## Promotion Rules
A student is promoted to the next year only after earning at least 70% of the
credits registered in the previous two semesters (DEMO_ONLY value). Students with
more backlogs are detained and must clear pending courses before progressing.
