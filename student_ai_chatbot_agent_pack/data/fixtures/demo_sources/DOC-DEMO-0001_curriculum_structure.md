# Demo Institute of Technology (DEMO_ONLY) — B.Tech CSE Curriculum Structure

## Program Overview
The B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Demo Institute of Technology
(DEMO_ONLY) is an eight-semester program spread across four academic years.

## Total Credits
The program requires a total of 160 credits for graduation (DEMO_ONLY value).
Credits are distributed across foundation courses, core courses, professional
electives, open electives, laboratories, and a final-year project.

## Credit Distribution
- Foundation and basic sciences: 32 credits
- Program core courses: 78 credits
- Professional electives: 18 credits
- Open electives: 12 credits
- Laboratories and practical work: 14 credits
- Project and internship: 6 credits

## Semester Model
Each academic year has two semesters. A regular semester carries between 18 and
22 credits. Students must clear prescribed courses before progressing per the
promotion rules document.
