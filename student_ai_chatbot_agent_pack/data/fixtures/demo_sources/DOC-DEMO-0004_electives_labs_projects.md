# Demo Institute of Technology (DEMO_ONLY) — Electives, Laboratories & Projects

## Professional Electives
The CSE program offers professional electives including Machine Learning
(DEMO-CS351), Computer Networks (DEMO-CS352), and Cloud Computing (DEMO-CS353).
Students choose 6 professional electives across semesters 6 to 8 (DEMO_ONLY).

## Open Electives
Open electives let students take courses from other departments. A student must
complete 12 credits of open electives (DEMO_ONLY value).

## How to Choose Electives
Elective registration opens two weeks before each semester. Seats are allotted
based on the registration order and prerequisites listed for each elective.

## Laboratory Information
Core laboratories include the Data Structures Lab (DEMO-CS281, 2 credits) and the
Operating Systems Lab (DEMO-CS282, 2 credits). Lab sessions run weekly for three
hours and require a minimum of 75% lab attendance (DEMO_ONLY value).

## Project Information
Final-year students complete a capstone project (DEMO-CS481) worth 6 credits
across semesters 7 and 8. Projects are done in teams of up to four students and
are evaluated by a departmental panel (DEMO_ONLY).
